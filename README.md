# 📊 Superstore Sales Analysis & Power BI Dashboard

**An Interactive Business Intelligence Project | Data Analytics Portfolio**

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard** is a data analytics and business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, product categories, customer segments, regional performance, and order trends.

The project transforms raw Superstore sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards and visualizations. It helps business stakeholders understand sales trends, identify profitable products, evaluate regional performance, and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, profit, and quantity sold.
- Monitor sales and profitability trends over time.
- Identify top-performing and low-performing product categories and sub-categories.
- Compare sales and profit across regions and customer segments.
- Understand the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Support business decision-making through interactive visualizations.

## 📂 Dataset Description

**Dataset:** Sample Superstore Sales Dataset

**File:** `sample_-_superstore.xls`

The dataset contains sales transaction information, including fields commonly used to analyze:

- Order Date and Ship Date
- Customer and Customer Segment
- Product Category and Sub-Category
- Sales and Quantity
- Discount and Profit
- Region, State, and City
- Shipping Mode

*Note: Actual fields and available records depend on the supplied dataset.*

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard can be used to monitor the following KPIs, depending on the measures implemented:

- **Total Sales:** Overall revenue generated from orders.
- **Total Profit:** Profit generated from sales.
- **Total Orders:** Number of unique orders.
- **Total Quantity Sold:** Total units sold.
- **Profit Margin (%):** Profit as a percentage of sales.
- **Average Order Value:** Average sales value per order.

- Compare product categories and sub-categories.
- Identify products contributing to sales and profit.
- Evaluate quantity sold and product performance.

### 4. Customer Segment Analysis
- Compare sales across customer segments.
- Understand which segments contribute most to revenue.
- Support customer-focused business decisions.

### 5. Regional Analysis
- Compare sales and profitability across geographical regions.
- Identify high-performing and underperforming locations.
- Support regional business planning.

## ⚙️ Project Workflow

1. **Data Collection:** Loaded the Superstore dataset from Excel.
2. **Data Preparation:** Inspected the dataset and prepared data using Power Query.
3. **Data Modeling:** Organized data for reporting and analysis, where applicable.
4. **DAX Calculations:** Created relevant measures for sales, profit, orders, and other KPIs, as implemented.
5. **Dashboard Development:** Built interactive visualizations and filters in Power BI.
6. **Business Analysis:** Used the dashboard to explore sales trends and performance across categories, segments, and regions.
